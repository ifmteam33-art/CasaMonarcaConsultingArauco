Who is Arauco México?

Celulosa Arauco y Constitución S.A. operates wood panel plants in Durango and Zitácuaro. Its revenues are denominated in MXN, while key inputs — urea and methanol — are quoted and paid in USD.

📊

The Risk

A depreciation of the MXN directly increases production costs. When the dollar strengthens, operating margins compress with no short-term ability to pass the cost increase on to the market.

🔄

The Solution

A Cross-Currency Swap locks in today the rate at which Arauco will deliver MXN and receive USD over 6 months, eliminating FX uncertainty across its raw material purchase cycle.

Executive Summary

Casa Monarca Consulting recommends implementing a MXN/USD Currency Swap with a notional of USD 2.0 million — representing Arauco México's residual unhedged chemical exposure after accounting for existing natural hedges (USD export revenues, in-house resin production, and local wood fiber sourcing). The swap matures in November 2026, covering the USMCA review window in July 2026, and locks in a rate of 18.10 MXN/USD on the unhedged portion of urea and methanol purchases.

Deliverable 4 — Hedging Strategy

Participating Forward
(Forward + Cross-Currency Swap)

Casa Monarca Consulting recommends a two-leg, zero-cost structure that locks Arauco México's MXN/USD exchange rate for its H2 2026 chemical procurement cycle while capturing Mexico's positive interest rate differential.

Arauco México's Existing Natural Hedges

Before designing any instrument, we identify the internal offsets that already reduce Arauco's net FX exposure. The strategy targets only the residual gap these hedges do not cover.

🇺🇸

USD Export Revenues

Arauco ships panels from Mexico to U.S. customers, generating USD inflows that naturally offset a portion of USD-denominated input costs at the subsidiary level.

🌲

Local Wood Fiber

Raw wood fiber is sourced domestically in Durango — a major pine-producing state — eliminating a significant USD-denominated input.

🏭

In-House Resin Plant

The Durango facility produces resins and formalin locally. Only specialty chemicals (urea, methanol) remain fully exposed to USD pricing.

👷

MXN Labor & Utilities

Labor, electricity, and leases are contracted in pesos. When MXN depreciates, these costs fall in USD terms — partially offsetting higher peso costs of imported inputs.

Why a Participating Forward (Forward + Swap)?

Arauco's exposure is fixed, predictable, and recurring — ideal for a forward lock. The Cross-Currency Swap captures the 2.84% p.a. MXN/USD rate differential (TIIE 6.50% vs SOFR 3.66%), already embedded in the forward price via Covered Interest Parity. Combined, the two legs produce a single effective rate of 17.6263 MXN/USD with zero upfront cost. Straddles and strangles were ruled out because Arauco needs cost certainty, not volatility exposure. Plain options add an upfront premium without improving the protection profile for a known, recurring obligation.
