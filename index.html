About This Report

Celulosa Arauco y Constitución S.A. (Arauco) is a Chilean forestry conglomerate and the primary wood panel producer in Mexico, operating plants in Durango and Zitácuaro. As part of its production process, Arauco México purchases urea and methanol — specialty chemicals priced in USD — while generating revenues in MXN. This currency mismatch creates a structural FX exposure that Casa Monarca Consulting has been engaged to analyze and hedge.

This report covers the macroeconomic context, risk landscape, and a detailed hedging strategy using a Synthetic Call — a combination of a Long Forward and a Long Put with K = F = 17.6263 — recommended for immediate implementation.

📊

The Risk

A depreciation of the MXN directly increases production costs. When the dollar strengthens, operating margins compress with no short-term ability to pass the cost increase on to the market.

🔄

The Solution

A Synthetic Call (Long Forward + Long Put, K = 17.6263) gives Arauco full protection against peso depreciation while preserving upside if the peso strengthens. Put premium: MXN 0.5344/USD · Total: MXN 5.13M · Break-even: 18.1607 MXN/USD.

Executive Summary

Casa Monarca Consulting recommends a Synthetic Call (Long Forward + Long Put) on USD 9.6M, covering Arauco México's full H2 2026 urea and methanol exposure. The forward leg locks in the rate at F = K = 17.6263 MXN/USD via CIP, while the put (Garman-Kohlhagen, σ = 10.5%) provides upside participation if the peso appreciates. Put premium: MXN 0.5344/USD (total MXN 5,130,240). Break-even spot at maturity: 18.1607 MXN/USD. The hedge matures December 4, 2026.

Macroeconomic Analysis

Key Macroeconomic Variables

Five key macroeconomic variables shape the FX outlook for Arauco México and inform our hedging strategy. Each is directly linked to the company's cost structure and risk exposure.

🏦

1. Interest Rate Differential

Banxico's benchmark rate stands at 6.50% (terminal, May 2026) vs. the Fed's 3.65% IORB. This 2.84% differential is the mathematical foundation of the forward rate via Covered Interest Parity — it determines how far F deviates from spot and the carry benefit Arauco captures through the swap leg.

💱

2. USD/MXN Exchange Rate

The peso has been volatile since 2024, ranging from 17.10 to 20.30 in analyst forecasts for end-2026. Citi's 35-bank consensus projects ~19.00, implying a 9.8% depreciation from today's 17.31. This is the primary risk driver for Arauco's input costs — every peso that depreciates increases the MXN cost of urea and methanol purchases.

📈

3. Inflation

Mexico's annual inflation reached 4.45% in April 2026 (INEGI), well above Banxico's 3% target. U.S. inflation is at 3.80% (BLS). The persistent inflationary gap constrains Banxico's ability to cut rates further and supports the MXN carry advantage — but also raises operating costs across Arauco's entire supply chain.

🌍

4. GDP Growth

Mexico's economy contracted 0.6% QoQ in Q1 2026 (INEGI), with Banxico revising its full-year forecast down to 1.1%. Weak domestic demand reduces Arauco's pricing power in the local panel market, making FX cost control even more critical — the company cannot easily pass through higher input costs to customers.

⚗️

5. Input Commodity Prices

Urea is trading at ~$420/ton (World Bank mid-2026) and methanol at ~$340/ton (Methanex Q4 2025/Q1 2026). Both are USD-denominated global commodities. Combined with MXN depreciation, these prices determine the total peso cost of Arauco's H2 2026 procurement — the exact exposure the hedge is designed to neutralize.

Deliverable 4 — Hedging Strategy

Synthetic Call
(Long Forward + Long Put)

Casa Monarca Consulting structures a Synthetic Call that replicates a call option on USD: the Long Forward locks Arauco's rate at K = 17.6263 MXN/USD, while the Long Put provides the right — not the obligation — to benefit if the peso appreciates below that strike.

Arauco México's Existing Natural Hedges

Before sizing any instrument, Casa Monarca identifies the internal offsets already reducing net FX exposure. The Synthetic Call targets only the residual unhedged gap.

🇺🇸

USD Export Revenues

Arauco ships panels from Mexico to U.S. customers, generating USD inflows that naturally offset a portion of USD-denominated input costs at the subsidiary level.

🌲

Local Wood Fiber

Raw wood fiber is sourced domestically in Durango — a major pine-producing state — eliminating a significant USD-denominated input from the exposure calculation.

🏭

In-House Resin Plant

The Durango facility produces resins and formalin locally. Only specialty chemicals (urea, methanol) remain fully exposed to USD global pricing.

👷

MXN Labor & Utilities

Labor, electricity, and leases contracted in pesos. When MXN depreciates these costs fall in USD terms — partially offsetting higher peso costs of imported inputs.

Why a Synthetic Call — and Why Not Alternatives

The Synthetic Call replicates a call option on USD: if the peso depreciates (S_T > K), the Long Forward pays off, covering the higher input cost. If the peso appreciates (S_T < K), the Long Put allows Arauco to exit the forward and transact at the better market rate. This gives full downside protection plus upside participation — unlike a plain forward which locks in the rate regardless, or a swap which has no optionality. The cost is the put premium (MXN 5.13M), a known, bounded outlay vs. an unbounded loss without hedging in a stress scenario.

Synthetic Call — Two-Leg Structure

LEG 1 — LONG FORWARD
Forward Contract

Arauco agrees today to buy USD 9.6M at K = 17.6263 MXN/USD on Dec 4, 2026. Fully eliminates depreciation risk above K. Zero upfront cost — OTC with bank.

Payoff = (S_T − K) × N if S_T > K
LEG 2 — LONG PUT (GK)
Put Option on USD/MXN

Arauco buys the right to sell USD at K = 17.6263 if peso appreciates below strike. Priced via Garman-Kohlhagen (σ = 10.5%, T = 0.5y). Premium: MXN 0.5344/USD.

Payoff = max(K − S_T, 0) × N − Premium
Combined payoff: max(S_T − K, 0) × N − Premium ≡ Long Call on USD · Break-even: S_T = 18.1607
Put Option Valuation — Garman-Kohlhagen Model
d₁ = [ln(S/K) + (r_d − r_f + σ²/2)·T] / (σ·√T)
d₁ = [ln(17.31/17.6263) + (0.065 − 0.0366 + 0.005513) × 0.5] / (0.105 × √0.5)
d₁ = [−0.01812 + 0.01696] / 0.07425 = −0.0156
d₂ = d₁ − σ·√T = −0.0156 − 0.07425 = −0.0899

Put = K·e−r_d·T·N(−d₂) − S·e−r_f·T·N(−d₁)
Put = 17.6263 × 0.96800 × 0.5358 − 17.31 × 0.98185 × 0.5062
Put = 9.1393 − 8.6049 = 0.5344 MXN/USD
